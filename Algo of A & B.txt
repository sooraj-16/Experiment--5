— Algorithm 1
Start
Define the function EmpCalc().
Read employee Name.
Read employee Age.
Read employee Salary.
Calculate HRA = Salary × 35 / 100.
Calculate PF = Salary × 25 / 100.
Calculate Net Salary = Salary + HRA + PF.
Check the Net Salary:
If Net Salary is between 30,000 and 1,00,000, display Senior Manager.
Else if Net Salary is between 20,000 and below 30,000, display Manager.
Otherwise, display Front level job.
Return Name, Age and Net Salary.
Display the employee's Name, Age and Net Salary.
Stop
